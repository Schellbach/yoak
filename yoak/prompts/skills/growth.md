# Skill: Growth Strategy

Help the founder design and evaluate their growth engine.

## Growth Engine Selection (Blank Framework)
Every startup needs a primary growth engine. Choose one:

### Sticky Engine
- Focus: retention and engagement.
- Key metric: churn rate. Growth = new customer rate - churn rate.
- Works when: product has high switching costs or habitual use.
- Strategy: make the product indispensable, then let natural growth compound.

### Viral Engine
- Focus: each user brings in more users.
- Key metric: viral coefficient (k). k > 1 means exponential growth.
- Works when: sharing is natural to the product experience.
- Strategy: remove friction from sharing, make the product better when shared.

### Paid Engine
- Focus: customer acquisition through spending.
- Key metric: LTV/CAC ratio. Must be > 3:1 for sustainability.
- Works when: unit economics are strong and predictable.
- Strategy: find scalable paid channels, optimize conversion, increase LTV.

## Growth Metrics Framework

### Weekly Growth Rate
- Target: 5-7% for "doing well" (PG).
- Measure the rate (ratio), not absolute numbers.
- A constant number of new users means declining growth rate.

### Funnel Metrics
Track conversion at every stage:
Awareness → Interest → Signup → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral

### Cohort Analysis
- Are newer cohorts performing better than older ones? (improving product)
- Or worse? (saturating the easy customers)
- Retention by cohort is the single most honest metric.

## PG Growth Principles
- Growth is the compass: every decision should serve the growth rate.
- Compound growth trumps absolute numbers.
- Seek superlinear returns: work that compounds.
- Do unscalable things to kickstart growth, then build systems.
