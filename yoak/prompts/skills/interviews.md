# Skill: User Research — Interview Script Generation & Synthesis

## Interview Script Generation

Generate a customer interview script based on the current hypothesis being tested.

### Problem Interview Template
For use during Customer Discovery when testing whether the problem exists.

**Opening (2 min):**
"Thanks for taking the time. I'm trying to understand how [target audience] handles [problem area]. There are no wrong answers — I'm here to learn from your experience."

**Context (3 min):**
- "Tell me about your role and how [problem area] fits into your work/life."
- "Walk me through a typical [relevant scenario]."

**Problem Exploration (10 min):**
- "What's the hardest part about [doing X]?"
- "Why is that hard?"
- "How often do you run into this?"
- "Can you tell me about the last time this happened?"
- "What did you do about it?"
- "What have you tried to solve this?"
- "What don't you love about those solutions?"

**Impact (3 min):**
- "What does this problem cost you?" (time, money, frustration, opportunity)
- "If this were solved perfectly, what would change for you?"

**Closing (2 min):**
- "Is there anything else about [problem area] that I should have asked about?"
- "Who else should I talk to about this?"

**Rules:**
- Do NOT pitch your solution during a problem interview.
- Listen more than you talk (80/20 rule).
- Follow the pain. When they mention something painful, go deeper.
- Take verbatim notes — their words, not your interpretation.

### Solution Interview Template
For use when you have an MVP to show.

**Opening:** Same as above.
**Context:** Brief recap of the problem (confirm it still resonates).
**Demo (5 min):** Show the MVP. Minimal explanation — let them react.
**Reaction (10 min):**
- "What's your first reaction?"
- "Which part would be most useful to you?"
- "What's confusing or missing?"
- "How does this compare to what you're using now?"
- "Would you use this? How often?"
- "Would you pay for this? How much would be fair?"
- "Would you recommend this to a colleague?"

## Interview Synthesis

After conducting interviews, synthesize findings:

1. **Pattern Recognition**: What themes appeared in 3+ interviews?
2. **Surprise Detection**: What did you hear that contradicted your assumptions?
3. **Verbatim Quotes**: Capture the most compelling customer language.
4. **Hypothesis Update**: For each BMC block, did interviews validate, invalidate, or raise new questions?
5. **Segment Refinement**: Did a more specific customer segment emerge?
6. **Priority Stack**: Based on interviews, what should be tested next?
