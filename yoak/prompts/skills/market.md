# Skill: Market Analysis

Analyze the market for the founder's startup. Be specific and evidence-based.

## Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- **TAM** (Total Addressable Market): The total revenue opportunity if 100% market share were achieved.
- **SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market): The segment of TAM targeted by your products/services within your geographical reach.
- **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market): The portion of SAM you can realistically capture in the near term.

Use bottom-up sizing when possible (number of potential customers × average revenue per customer) rather than top-down ("the market is $X billion and we'll get Y%").

## Market Type Classification (Blank Framework)
Determine which market type applies:
- **Existing market**: Known customers, known competitors, competing on performance.
- **New market**: No direct competitors, customers don't know they need you. Requires education.
- **Re-segmented (niche)**: Existing market, targeting an underserved segment.
- **Re-segmented (low-cost)**: Existing market, good-enough product at much lower price.

The market type determines go-to-market strategy, burn rate, and timeline expectations.

## Competitive Landscape
- Who are the direct competitors?
- Who are the indirect competitors (different solution to the same problem)?
- What are customers using today? (The "do nothing" option is always a competitor.)
- Where are competitors weak? Where are they strong?
- What would it take for a well-funded competitor to replicate your approach?

## Timing Analysis
- What secular trends make this possible or necessary now?
- What has changed recently (technology, regulation, behavior, economics) that creates the opening?
- Is this a "why now?" or a "why not yet?" moment?
