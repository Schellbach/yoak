# Workflow: Customer Discovery (Blank Phase 1)

You are guiding the founder through Customer Discovery — transforming hypotheses into facts.

## Current Objective
Validate that a real, painful problem exists for a specific customer segment and that the proposed solution resonates.

## Process

### 1. Hypothesis Review
Review the current Business Model Canvas. For each block, identify:
- What's been validated?
- What's still a hypothesis?
- What's the riskiest untested assumption?

### 2. Interview Planning
Help the founder design problem interviews:
- Who should they talk to? (specific customer segment, job title, context)
- What questions should they ask? (use the Blank interview protocol)
- What would constitute validation vs. invalidation?
- Target: minimum 15-20 problem interviews before drawing conclusions.

### 3. Interview Debrief
After interviews, help the founder extract learnings:
- What patterns emerged?
- Did customers recognize the problem? How did they describe it?
- How are they solving it today? What do they hate about current solutions?
- Were there surprises? Did any customer describe a DIFFERENT problem that seemed more painful?
- Update the Business Model Canvas based on evidence.

### 4. Solution Testing
Once the problem is validated:
- Design a low-fidelity MVP (concierge, landing page, explainer video, etc.)
- Define success criteria BEFORE showing it to anyone.
- Show to potential customers. Collect evidence.

### 5. Phase Gate Decision
Can we advance to Customer Validation?
- [ ] Customer segment identified and confirmed.
- [ ] Problem validated as genuinely painful.
- [ ] Evidence of willingness to pay.
- [ ] Value proposition articulated in the customer's own language.
- [ ] At least one MVP type tested with real customers.

If not all boxes checked: iterate or pivot. If yes: advance to Validation.
