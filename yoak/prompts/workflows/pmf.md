# Workflow: Product/Market Fit Assessment

You are assessing whether the startup has achieved Product/Market Fit.

## Qualitative Assessment
Ask about each signal:

| Signal | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Customers buying as fast as you can produce | ? | |
| Usage growing organically (word of mouth) | ? | |
| Customers upset at idea of product disappearing | ? | |
| Press/analysts reaching out to you | ? | |
| Hiring because demand exceeds capacity | ? | |
| Revenue growing without proportional marketing spend | ? | |
| Customer referrals are meaningful acquisition source | ? | |

## Quantitative Assessment

### Sean Ellis Test
Survey current users: "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?"
- Very disappointed: ___% (need 40%+ for P/M Fit)
- Somewhat disappointed: ___%
- Not disappointed: ___%

### Retention
- Do retention curves flatten or decline to zero?
- Month-over-month retention rate: ___%
- Cohort analysis trend: improving / stable / declining

### Growth Metrics
- Weekly growth rate: ___% (target 5-7%)
- Organic vs. paid ratio: ___
- Viral coefficient (k): ___ (>0.5 = strong organic pull)

### Unit Economics
- CAC: $___
- LTV: $___
- LTV/CAC ratio: ___ (target >3:1)
- Monthly churn: ___% (target <5% SMB, <2% enterprise)
- Time to value: ___ (trending down = good)

## Diagnosis

### Strong P/M Fit (advance to Customer Creation)
- 40%+ "very disappointed" on Ellis test
- Retention curves flatten
- LTV/CAC > 3:1
- Organic growth present

### Partial P/M Fit (iterate)
- Fit exists in a subsegment but not broadly
- Some strong signals but inconsistent
- Action: narrow focus to strongest segment, deepen the fit there first

### No P/M Fit (return to Discovery or Validation)
- Most signals negative
- Growth proportional to spend
- High churn
- Divergent feature requests (no pattern)
- Action: revisit hypotheses, consider pivot
