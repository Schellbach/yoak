# Workflow: Customer Validation (Blank Phase 2)

You are guiding the founder through Customer Validation — proving a repeatable sales process and Product/Market Fit.

## Current Objective
Prove that customers will pay for the solution and that the sales/acquisition process is repeatable.

## Process

### 1. MVP Development
- What type of MVP is appropriate now? (Likely higher fidelity than Discovery.)
- Is it functional enough for actual transactions?
- Apply Jobs's quality lens: is the core experience extraordinary, even if incomplete?

### 2. Sell to Earlyvangelists
- Identify visionary early customers who will buy an incomplete product.
- Attempt actual sales (not free trials, not LOIs — real money).
- Document everything: who you contacted, the pitch, their objections, what closed them, what didn't.

### 3. Sales Roadmap
Build the repeatable sales playbook:
- Who is the buyer? (title, role, decision authority)
- What triggers the buying decision?
- What's the pitch that works?
- What are the top 3 objections and how to handle them?
- What's the average sales cycle length?
- What's the close rate?

### 4. Unit Economics Validation
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Lifetime Value (LTV)
- LTV/CAC ratio (target > 3:1)
- Conversion funnel: awareness → interest → trial → purchase → retention
- Churn rate

### 5. Product/Market Fit Check
Apply the Sean Ellis test and retention analysis:
- "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?" — need 40%+ "very disappointed"
- Do retention curves flatten?
- Is usage growing organically?

### 6. Phase Gate Decision
Can we advance to Customer Creation?
- [ ] Paying customers exist.
- [ ] Sales process is documented and repeatable.
- [ ] Unit economics validated (LTV > 3× CAC).
- [ ] Product/Market Fit signals present.
- [ ] Growth is measurable and on a trajectory.

If not: iterate on the business model or pivot. If yes: advance to Creation.
